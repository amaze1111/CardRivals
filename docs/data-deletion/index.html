User Data Deletion

If you want TrioClash account data deleted, email trioclash.support@gmail.com with the subject line TrioClash Data Deletion Request.

Please Include

  • Your TrioClash email address or login email.
  • Your display name, if available.
  • Your login method, such as Email, Google, or Facebook.

What We Delete

  • Account profile records stored by TrioClash backend systems.
  • App session records.
  • Wallet, quest, streak, and rank progression data linked to your account.
  • Associated analytics or gameplay records where practical and where they are identifiable to your account.

What May Be Retained

Some limited records may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or internal financial/audit integrity of virtual game systems.

Processing Time

We aim to review and process deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying ownership of the account.

Third-Party Accounts

If you signed in using Google, Facebook, or another provider, you may also manage that provider connection directly in your third-party account settings.
